# Standard Photo Labels: RRO Daily Observation

Built from the 456 captioned photos in the CompanyCam projects for BOK Auto Bank, BOK Norman and Hollis High School (May to October 2026), and from the daily observation, roof monitoring and RRO field-record reports in those three project folders. The building envelope labels were added after a review of the BOK ROC photos from 10/19/2026.

## Caption format

```
LABEL - Location: what the photo shows, measured against what. Status
```

- **LABEL**: one word from the list below. It always comes first, so captions sort and group by label.
- **Location**: roof area, wall or item number (West roof, Lane 3, RTU-2, Pitch pan 1).
- **What it shows**: the fact or the number, not the activity ("Base flashing 6 in." rather than "Flashing in process").
- **Status** (optional, last word): `OK`, `Deviation`, `Open`, `Closed` or `Review` (a decision for the owner's representative).

Examples:

```
Flashing - West roof, NW corner: base flashing 6 in., below NRCA 8 in. min; slope drains away. Review
Pitchpan - Pitch pan 2, center drive-thru: filled to top. Closed
Corner - East section, corner 1: folded by hand, no corner detail. Deviation
Ponding - North roof, center: dry 48 h after Sunday rain. OK
Grade - South slab at elevation change: re-poured section set high; hump stops drainage to the edge. Review
```

## What the review found

1. **Captions describe activity, not the point.** About 45 captions say what the crew was doing ("Flashing in process", "Detail work", "Roof in process") with no location or measurement. The label plus a location fixes most of these.
2. **Bare measurements.** Captions such as "53 in. wide" or "12 in. on the south side" do not name the item measured. A measurement always follows a label (`Flashing - ... 12 in.`).
3. **The same caption repeats across a photo series.** "Tear off the area over lanes 3 and 4" appears on four photos. Keep the caption and add "(2 of 4)".
4. **Narrative belongs in the report body.** 28 captions run 30 words or more, most of them on Auto Bank. Keep captions to about 20 words and move the story to Work observed or Deviations.
5. **Status is buried in the sentence.** "This is a deviation", "Again this closes an open item" and "So need to check with Josh" become the status word at the end: `Deviation`, `Closed`, `Review`.
6. **Many photos have no caption.** 541 of the 997 photos pulled have none: 253 uploaded by the roofing crews and 288 by Chad and Josh. Only photos tagged Inspection for the report need a caption, so untagged extras are fine.
